# Privacy operations (RA 10173, the Data Privacy Act)

## Status

Working notes for Primolar's operator and for counsel, measured 2026-10-19 (B13c2a). **Nothing here is legal advice.**
Every line marked *confirm with counsel* is a placeholder that stays switched off or conservative in the code until
counsel answers (B13c2 `decisions.md` U1, C1–C5). The data inventory below is generated from the API's retention
schedule and held to it by a test.

## Roles

- **Each clinic is the personal information controller** for its patients' records. **Primolar is its processor**
  (the DPA template's "Roles", `packages/shared/src/domain/legal.ts`). Primolar is the controller only of its own data:
  clinic accounts, sign-ups, its subscriptions and the operator's trail.
- Children's dental and medical records are **sensitive personal information**.
- Primolar's DPO: `PRIMOLAR_DPO` in `domain/legal.ts` (its email is itself marked for counsel).

## Registration

- Whether each clinic, and Primolar, must register its data processing system and DPO with the National Privacy
  Commission depends on the NPC's thresholds (commonly summarised as 250 or more employees, or processing sensitive
  personal information of 1,000 or more people): *confirm with counsel* for a typical clinic and for Primolar.
- Who files: each clinic for its own patients' records; Primolar for its own processing. When: before the first paying
  clinics go live (*confirm with counsel*).

## Retention

- **Enforced by the API's daily jobs** (Asia/Manila, on the one instance running background work): `auth.sweep`
  (every 15 minutes: sessions, rate-limit windows), `idempotency.sweep` (hourly), `outbox.prune` (03:10),
  `signups.purge-rejected-files` (03:15), `portal.prune-links` (03:20: links 30 days past their expiry; not a legal
  threshold, the rows are dead tokens) and `operator-audit.prune` (03:25).
- **Never deleted by the system** until counsel gives periods: the child's clinical record, money records (the tax
  law's period is proposed as 10 years) and the clinic's audit trail. How long a child's record is kept after the last
  visit is a legal threshold (for minors often counted from the age of majority): *confirm with counsel*.
- **The operator trail** is pruned only when `OPERATOR_AUDIT_KEEP_DAYS` is set (unset keeps it forever; the minimum is
  365; proposed 1826, five years): *confirm with counsel* before setting it. It is not in `packages/infra/cdk.json` yet.
- **Erasure** (B13c2b) will restrict a record at once and anonymise it only when its legal retention ends; that job
  ships **off** (`RETENTION_ERASURE=false`) and refuses to run while the periods are placeholders.

## Security measures

- A login per person, access by role (`ROLE_PERMISSIONS`, deny by default), guardian contacts only to roles that need
  them, and a parent only to their own children.
- The audit trail: every read of a child's record and every change, readable by the Owner in Settings → Privacy
  (`GET /audit-log`, also open to admins through the API); refused requests are logged as `denied`.
- Passwords hashed with argon2id; httpOnly, secure session cookies with idle and absolute timeouts; rate-limited
  sign-in.
- TLS between the browser and the load balancer. No TLS between the API and the database yet (B13b I5).
- AWS ap-southeast-1: RDS storage encryption, S3 server-side encryption with Block Public Access, files opened only
  through 5-minute private links that are checked and logged each time.
- RDS backups with point-in-time restore kept 30 days, and a restore drill (`packages/infra/runbooks/restore-drill.md`).
- Logs never carry patient data (the redaction list, `PERSONAL_FIELDS` in `apps/api/src/logging/logger.ts`, tested).

## Sub-processors

- **Amazon Web Services** (ap-southeast-1): compute, database, files and logs.
- **Vercel**: serves the web app; it stores no patient data (every record comes from the API).
- **Semaphore**: sends texts; receives the guardian's mobile number and the text's words.
- Each needs a data processing agreement on file: *confirm with counsel*.

## Requests

- **Access and portability:** a copy of the child's records (`POST /patients/:id/exports`, a JSON file, logged as an
  export). Staff download it from the child's Privacy card; a parent from the portal's Privacy page (what the portal
  shows: no chart, notes, growth, visits or documents; the clinic gives the full copy on request).
- **Correction:** staff edit the record; chart entries and payments are corrected by a new entry, never overwritten.
- **The register:** the Owner (or an admin, through the API) logs every request in Settings → Privacy → Privacy
  requests: who asked, how, when, about which child. It is closed with what was done. Logged and closed requests are
  in the audit trail ("Privacy request").
- **The deadline to answer** a request is a legal threshold. It is counted in working days (Monday to Friday;
  holidays aren't skipped, so it errs early) from `PRIVACY_REQUEST_DUE_WORKING_DAYS`, default 15: *confirm with
  counsel*. Late requests show as Overdue.
- **Erasure:** approving an erasure request **restricts** the child's record at once. It leaves every list, search and
  the parents' portal, photos and reminders turn off, queued texts are withdrawn, and only the Owner and admins can
  open it (every other attempt is refused and logged). Clinic-wide lists (calendar, documents, collections) still name
  the child; the due list leaves them out, and no one can book them. It is anonymised only when its legal retention ends (B13c2b-2:
  the job ships off until counsel confirms the period). Approval waits until the child has no upcoming appointment or
  pending request.
- **Objection:** logged and answered in the register. Stopping a use (reminders, photos) is the child's privacy
  choices.
- **Parents** ask the clinic (the portal's Privacy page says so). There is no self-serve request yet.

## Breach procedure

1. **Detect:** the API's alarms, the audit log (refused requests, unusual exports), reports from staff or parents.
2. **Contain:** turn off the login or portal access, rotate secrets, pause the clinic if needed.
3. **Assess:** what data, whose, how many people, and the likely harm.
4. **Notify:** Primolar tells the affected clinic without undue delay; the clinic, as controller, notifies the NPC and
   the affected families within the legal deadline (72 hours from knowledge under NPC Circular 16-03 as commonly
   summarised): *confirm with counsel*.
5. **Record** the incident, what was done and what changes, whether or not it was notifiable.
