Wait, I don't understand where you've got to. Re-pitch your last message:

- Start with one or two sentences of context: what we were doing and why this matters now.
- Use short sentences and common words, one idea per sentence (ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English).
- Use this project's own terms, as `CONVENTIONS.md`, the requirements register or the `decisions.md`
  for this work define them. Do not invent new names for things that already have one.
- Say exactly what you need from me, if anything.
