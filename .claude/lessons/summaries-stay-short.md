**Lesson:** When the user asks to summarize or explain in plain terms, answer in two or three short lines plus the single action they need to take.

**Why:** "Summarize" here means a glance-length answer, not a shorter report. A sectioned summary with numbered steps was rejected outright, and the rewrite cost an extra turn.

**Do:**
- One line for where things stand, one for what changed, one for the reply or action needed.
- No headings, no lists of more than three items, no background the user didn't ask for.
- Offer detail on request instead of including it.
